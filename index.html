Integrative Clinical Modalities

My Eclectic Therapeutic Approach

No two people are identical. An "eclectic modality" means I do not force you into a rigid, one-size-fits-all box. Instead, I carefully tailor proven clinical modalities to match your unique personality, emotional pace, and cultural context.

CBT Cognitive

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy

Examining the Link Between Thoughts, Feelings & Actions

When stressed, our minds jump to catastrophic conclusions. CBT teaches you to catch automatic negative assumptions, test if they are facts, and build balanced, realistic perspectives.

Best for: anxiety, overthinking, panic, sleep troubles.
ACT Mindfulness

Acceptance & Commitment Therapy

Embracing Life & Moving Toward What Matters

Instead of fighting or exhausting yourself trying to eliminate difficult feelings, ACT helps you make room for them while committing to actions aligned with your deepest personal values.

Best for: chronic stress, feeling stuck, grief, existential uncertainty.
REBT Action-Oriented

Rational Emotive Behavior Therapy

Dismantling Rigid "Musts" and Demands

Created by Albert Ellis, REBT targets irrational demands like "I must never fail" or "People must always approve of me," replacing them with flexible, self-accepting preferences.

Best for: perfectionism, imposter syndrome, anger, frustration tolerance.
SFBT Strengths-Based

Solution-Focused Brief Therapy

Building on Strengths & Preferred Future

Instead of getting bogged down analyzing every past wound, SFBT identifies what has worked in your life, amplifies your resilience, and designs actionable steps for momentum.

Best for: life transitions, goal setting, overcoming inertia.
Rogerian Person-Centered

Rogerian / Person-Centered Care

Unconditional Regard & Empathy

You will never receive cold, detached lectures. Carl Rogers' person-centered philosophy provides deep, genuine empathy and non-judgmental acceptance so your innate healing capacity can flourish.

Best for: shame, emotional isolation, feeling unheard, vulnerability.
Gottman Couples Method

The Gottman Method

Science-Backed Relational Healing

Based on 40+ years of research with thousands of couples. We target the 4 Horsemen (criticism, defensiveness, contempt, stonewalling) and practice practical conversational antidotes to rebuild trust and intimacy.

Best for: marital conflict, communication barriers, intimacy disconnect.
Sikolohiyang Pilipino Culturally Grounded

Filipino Psychology & Psychoeducation

Honoring Kapwa, Utang na Loob & the Filipino Reality

Western therapy often misunderstands the collective Filipino family. Vince integrates indigenous concepts like kapwa (shared identity) and explores healthy adult boundaries with parents, extended family, or partners without invalidating filial love, respect, or cultural identity.

Best for: family boundary strain, breadwinner pressure, navigating "tampo", and cultural identity.
