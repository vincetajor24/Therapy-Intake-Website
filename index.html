Integrative Psychological Framework

My Modalities: An Eclectic Approach

An "eclectic approach" means Vince doesn’t force you into a one-size-fits-all formula. Because your story, challenges, and cultural background are unique, sessions thoughtfully blend the most effective evidence-based modalities tailored to what helps you heal.

CBT Evidence-Based

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy

Untangling Thoughts, Feelings & Actions

When stress hits, our minds produce automatic negative thoughts (jumping to worst cases or assuming failure). In CBT, we put those thoughts on trial against objective reality to replace cognitive distortions with calm, balanced beliefs.

Best for: anxiety spirals, depressive thoughts, panic, catastrophizing.
ACT Evidence-Based

Acceptance & Commitment Therapy

Dropping the Internal Tug-of-War

Suppressing difficult feelings often makes them push back harder. ACT fosters psychological flexibility: making mindful room for tough emotions without letting them control you, while taking committed steps toward what matters most.

Best for: chronic stress, avoidance, feeling stuck, value realignment.
REBT Evidence-Based

Rational Emotive Behavior Therapy

Challenging Rigid Demands & "Musts"

REBT identifies toxic perfectionism and rigid self-demands ("I must never fail", "Everyone must approve of me"). We transform rigid demands into healthy preferences and unconditional self-acceptance.

Best for: perfectionism, imposter syndrome, fear of judgment.
SFBT Evidence-Based

Solution-Focused Brief Therapy

Building on Strengths & Preferred Future

Instead of getting bogged down analyzing every past wound, SFBT identifies what has worked in your life, amplifies your resilience, and designs actionable steps for momentum.

Best for: life transitions, goal setting, overcoming inertia.
Rogerian Person-Centered

Rogerian / Person-Centered Care

Unconditional Regard & Empathy

You will never receive cold, detached lectures. Carl Rogers' person-centered philosophy provides deep, genuine empathy and non-judgmental acceptance so your innate healing capacity can flourish.

Best for: shame, emotional isolation, feeling unheard, vulnerability.
Gottman Couples Method

The Gottman Method

Science-Backed Relational Healing

Based on 40+ years of research with thousands of couples. We target the 4 Horsemen (criticism, defensiveness, contempt, stonewalling) and practice practical conversational antidotes to rebuild trust and intimacy.

Best for: marital conflict, communication barriers, intimacy disconnect.
Sikolohiyang Pilipino Culturally Grounded

Filipino Psychology & Psychoeducation

Honoring Kapwa, Utang na Loob & the Filipino Reality

Western therapy often misunderstands the collective Filipino family. Vince integrates indigenous concepts like kapwa (shared identity) and explores healthy adult boundaries with parents or partners without invalidating filial love, respect, or cultural identity.

Best for: family boundary strain, breadwinner pressure, navigating "tampo", and cultural identity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers regarding SeriousMD sessions, session duration, and AI transcription (Heidi Health).

Standard individual psychotherapy and sexual wellness consultations are 50 to 55 minutes. For marriage and couples counseling, sessions are 80 to 90 minutes to ensure both partners have balanced time to be heard without rushing.
You will receive a private, encrypted SeriousMD telehealth link via email and SMS prior to your appointment. You can join from your smartphone, tablet, or laptop in a quiet, confidential location.
To provide 100% undivided eye contact without constantly typing on a keyboard, Vince utilizes Heidi Health, a HIPAA-compliant, encrypted AI clinical scribe for note-taking. It does not train models on client audio. You are free to agree or decline on the intake form with zero impact on care.
While SeriousMD virtual sessions are the primary channel, in-person consultations are available at partner clinics: Mapagkalinga Psychological Services in Taytay, Rizal (Saturdays) and Aurahealth Rehab East Clinic at Robinsons Metroeast (Fridays).
